💣 THE 10 ENEMY CANNONBALLS (HIGH-BUDGET TRAPS)

  • 1. The 20-Language & Paid Press Illusion: Projects launching on day one with broken translations in 20 languages and paid media banners to fake legitimacy.
  • 2. Fake Countdowns & Shifting Dates: Countdown timers ('last 2 hours!') that reset back to zero to maintain constant psychological pressure.
  • 3. Exclusive Whitelists, Fake KYC & Imposter Founders: VIP spots dominated by insider wallets, bought devices or stolen biometrics used to bypass KYC checks, and fake 'owner' accounts running bogus giveaways to use everyday buyers as exit liquidity.
  • 4. Presale Bonuses (Pure Dilution): Offering '50% extra tokens' inflates circulating supply and crushes real value and liquidity the second the market opens.
  • 5. Unrealistic APY (10% Stables / 60%+ Token): Unsustainable yields on private websites and infinite token printing that guarantees future sell pressure.
  • 6. Inflated Buys & The Claim Gas Trap: Bots and whales pay $100 in gas to claim and dump thousands in second one, while the $20 buyer gets trapped unable to cover claim fees.
  • 7. Fake 'Tier-1' Listings & Closed Websites: Misleading silhouettes of major exchanges only to list on their own closed website, avoiding open smart contracts so funds never leave their hands.
  • 8. Paid Audits on the Token, Not the Vault: Auditing a basic ERC-20 template while leaving the presale, staking, or vault contract holding your money completely unaudited.
  • 9. Manual DEX Listing & Empty Pool (< 60%): Creators who own the manual pool charge up to 1% fee on every trade while setting high prices with minimal liquidity.
  • 10. Bot-Inflated Communities & Censorship: Groups with thousands of fake accounts shouting '¡compra ahora!' donde banean al instante a cualquiera que haga una pregunta real sobre liquidez o código.
